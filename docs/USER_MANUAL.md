## 1. Getting Started

### 1.1 Creating an Account

1. Open your browser and navigate to the AgroLink platform URL (or download the mobile app).
2. Click **Join Us** or **Get Started** on the homepage.
3. Fill in the registration form:
   - Full name
   - Email address or phone number
   - Password (minimum 8 characters)
   - Select your primary role (Farmer, Buyer, Cooperative, Logistics Partner, Warehouse Manager, or Investor)
4. Accept the Terms & Conditions and click **Create Account**.
5. A verification email (or SMS for phone sign-up) is sent. Click the link or enter the OTP to verify your account.

> **Tip:** Your role determines which features are visible on your dashboard. You can request a role change later from your account settings.

---

### 1.2 Logging In

1. Click **Login** in the top navigation bar.
2. Enter your registered email/phone and password.
3. Click **Sign In**.
4. If you forget your password, click **Forgot Password**, enter your email, and follow the reset link sent to your inbox.

---

### 1.3 Choosing Your Role

During registration, select the role that best describes you:

| Role | Choose this if you… |
|------|---------------------|
| Farmer | Grow crops or raise livestock and want to sell produce |
| Buyer | Purchase agricultural produce for personal or commercial use |
| Cooperative | Represent a farmers' cooperative or association |
| Logistics Partner | Provide transport services for farm produce |
| Warehouse Manager | Operate a storage facility and offer warehouse bookings |
| Investor | Want to fund agricultural projects and earn returns |

Admins are created internally and are not selectable during self-registration.

---

### 1.4 Language Selection

AgroLink supports multiple display languages.

1. Look for the **language toggle** (globe icon) in the top navigation bar.
2. Click it and select your preferred language from the dropdown.
3. The interface updates immediately without a page reload.

## 2. Dashboard Overview

After logging in, you are taken to your personalised dashboard. The layout varies slightly by role, but the main elements are consistent:

| Area | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Top Navigation** | Logo, notification bell, language switcher, user avatar/menu |
| **Sidebar** | Quick links to all sections available to your role |
| **Summary Cards** | At-a-glance metrics (total sales, wallet balance, active orders, etc.) |
| **Recent Activity** | Latest transactions, orders, or alerts |
| **Quick Actions** | Prominent buttons for the most common tasks for your role |

The dashboard loads real-time data. Refresh the page if figures appear stale.

## 3. Farmer Guide

### 3.1 Listing a Product

1. From the sidebar, go to **Marketplace → Add Product** (or **Product Listing**).
2. Complete the product form:
   - **Product name** — e.g. "White Maize, Dried"
   - **Category** — Grains, Vegetables, Fruits, Livestock, etc.
   - **Quantity available** — in kg, tonnes, bags, or units
   - **Unit price** — in Naira (₦)
   - **Location** — state and local government area
   - **Photos** — upload at least one clear photo of the produce
   - **Description** — variety, moisture content, harvest date, etc.
3. Click **Submit Listing**.
4. Your listing enters a review queue. Once approved by an admin, it becomes visible to buyers.

> **Best practice:** Check the [Crop Price Intelligence](#33-checking-crop-prices) page before setting a price to ensure you are competitive.

---

### 3.2 Managing Orders

1. Go to **Orders** or **Order Management** in the sidebar.
2. The orders list shows status badges: **Pending**, **Confirmed**, **Dispatched**, **Delivered**, **Cancelled**.
3. Click any order row to view its details — buyer information, delivery address, agreed price, and payment status.
4. **Confirm** an order when you are ready to fulfil it.
5. Once goods are collected by a logistics partner or shipped, the status updates to **Dispatched** automatically.
6. Mark an order **Delivered** when confirmed by the buyer or logistics partner.

---

### 3.3 Checking Crop Prices

1. Go to **Prices** in the sidebar.
2. Select a **crop** (e.g. Maize, Rice, Cassava) and a **location** (state/region).
3. The platform returns:
   - Recommended selling price
   - Best-selling location (market with highest demand)
   - Demand trend: **Rising**, **Stable**, or **Softening**
4. Use these signals to time your listings and negotiate better prices.

---

### 3.4 Requesting Logistics

1. Go to **Logistics** in the sidebar and click **Request Transport**.
2. Fill in:
   - Pickup location (farm address)
   - Delivery destination
   - Produce type and estimated weight/volume
   - Preferred pickup date and time
3. Submit the request. It is assigned to an available logistics partner.
4. Track the status in **Logistics → My Requests**: Pending → Assigned → In Transit → Delivered.
5. You receive an in-app notification at each status change.

---

### 3.5 Booking Warehouse Storage

1. Go to **Warehouse** in the sidebar and click **Book Storage**.
2. Browse available warehouses — each shows location, capacity, cost per tonne, and available dates.
3. Select a warehouse and specify:
   - Produce type
   - Quantity (tonnes or bags)
   - Start and end dates
4. Confirm the booking. A warehouse receipt is generated and visible under **My Bookings**.
5. To release stored inventory, click **Release** on the booking record.

---

### 3.6 Applying for a Loan

1. Go to **Loan Application** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Apply for Loan** and fill in:
   - Loan amount (₦)
   - Purpose (e.g. seeds, fertiliser, equipment)
   - Farm size (hectares)
   - Requested repayment term (months)
3. Your cooperative rating and sales history on the platform contribute to the assessment score.
4. Submit the application. The status updates to **Under Review**.
5. Track progress under **Loans → My Applications**: Pending → Under Review → Approved / Rejected.
6. Approved funds are credited to your AgroLink wallet.

---

### 3.7 Grading Produce

1. Go to **Grading** in the sidebar.
2. Submit a grading request for a batch of produce:
   - Select product / crop type
   - Enter quantity
   - Describe quality indicators (colour, moisture, defects)
3. An assigned grader reviews and assigns an official grade (Grade A, B, C, or Reject).
4. The grade is attached to your product listing and boosts buyer confidence.

---

### 3.8 Wallet & Withdrawals

1. Go to **Dashboard → Wallet** (or the wallet icon on the top bar).
2. The wallet screen shows:
   - **Available balance** — funds ready for withdrawal
   - **Pending balance** — funds held in escrow for active orders
   - **Transaction history** — all credits and debits with timestamps
3. To withdraw funds:
   - Click **Withdraw**.
   - Enter the amount and your bank account details (or a previously saved account).
   - Confirm the withdrawal. Funds are processed via Paystack or Flutterwave and typically arrive within 1–2 business days.

## 4. Buyer Guide

### 4.1 Browsing the Marketplace

1. Go to **Marketplace** in the navigation.
2. Use the filter panel to narrow results by:
   - Crop / product category
   - Location / state
   - Price range
   - Minimum quantity
   - Grade (if graded)
3. Click any product card to view full details, photos, farmer profile, and price history.

---

### 4.2 Placing an Order

1. On a product page, enter the **quantity** you want to purchase.
2. Review the order summary (unit price × quantity + logistics estimate).
3. Click **Place Order**.
4. Select your delivery address and preferred payment method.
5. Confirm. You receive an order confirmation notification.

---

### 4.3 Tracking an Order

1. Go to **Orders** in the sidebar.
2. Find the order by reference number or date.
3. Click the order to see its current status and logistics updates in a timeline view.
4. Contact the farmer or logistics partner via the in-app message link if you need to follow up.

---

### 4.4 Making a Payment

AgroLink supports the following payment methods:

| Method | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Paystack** | Card, bank transfer, USSD |
| **Flutterwave** | Card, mobile money, bank transfer |
| **Wallet balance** | Deducted instantly from your AgroLink wallet |

1. At checkout, select your payment method.
2. Follow the on-screen prompts for the chosen gateway.
3. On successful payment, you are redirected to the **Payment Success** confirmation page.
4. A payment receipt is emailed to your registered address.

## 5. Cooperative Guide

### 5.1 Registering a Cooperative

1. Go to **Cooperatives** in the sidebar and click **Register Cooperative**.
2. Provide:
   - Cooperative name and registration number
   - Registered state and LGA
   - Chairperson / contact person details
   - Number of members
3. Upload supporting documents (CAC certificate or equivalent).
4. Submit for admin approval.

---

### 5.2 Managing Members

1. Go to **Cooperatives → My Cooperative → Members**.
2. To add a member, click **Add Member** and enter their AgroLink account email or phone number.
3. To remove a member, click the trash icon next to their name and confirm.
4. Member transaction histories on AgroLink feed into the cooperative's collective credit score, which benefits all loan applicants within the group.

---

## 6. Logistics Partner Guide

### 6.1 Accepting a Dispatch Request

1. Go to **Logistics → Available Requests**.
2. Review open requests — each shows pickup location, destination, produce type, weight, and offered rate.
3. Click **Accept** on a request you can fulfil.
4. The request status changes to **Assigned** and the farmer is notified.

---

### 6.2 Updating Transit Status

1. Go to **Logistics → My Dispatches**.
2. Select the active dispatch.
3. Click **Update Status** and choose the appropriate stage:
   - **In Transit** — goods collected and on the way
   - **Delivered** — goods successfully delivered to the buyer
   - **Cancelled** — dispatch could not be completed (add a reason)
4. Each status update triggers notifications to the farmer and buyer.

---

## 7. Warehouse Manager Guide

### 7.1 Managing Storage Capacity

1. Go to **Warehouse → My Facility**.
2. Update available capacity, storage rates, and accepted produce types.
3. Mark slots as **Unavailable** during maintenance periods to prevent new bookings.

---

### 7.2 Confirming and Releasing Bookings

1. Go to **Warehouse → Bookings**.
2. Incoming booking requests appear with a **Confirm** or **Decline** action.
3. Confirm a booking to generate a warehouse receipt for the farmer.
4. When a farmer requests a release, click **Release** to update inventory and close the booking record.

---

## 8. Investor Guide

### 8.1 Browsing Opportunities

1. Go to **Invest** or **Investor Portal** in the navigation.
2. Browse available agricultural investment opportunities — each listing shows:
   - Project description and location
   - Target amount and amount raised so far
   - Expected return (%) and investment term
   - Risk rating
3. Click a listing for detailed project information, farmer/operator profile, and historical performance data.

---

### 8.2 Monitoring Returns

1. Go to **Investor → My Portfolio**.
2. View all active investments, their current value, and projected maturity dates.
3. Completed investments show confirmed returns credited to your wallet.
4. Download investment statements for record-keeping.

## 9. Admin Guide

> Admin accounts are provisioned internally. Contact the platform team to obtain admin credentials.

### 9.1 Approving Users

1. Go to **Admin → Users**.
2. Filter by **Pending Approval**.
3. Review the user's submitted details and documents.
4. Click **Approve** or **Reject** (add a rejection reason).
5. The user receives an email notification.

---

### 9.2 Approving Products

1. Go to **Admin → Products**.
2. Pending listings appear at the top.
3. Review product details, photos, and pricing.
4. Click **Approve** to publish the listing, or **Reject** with a note.

---

### 9.3 Managing Loans

1. Go to **Admin → Loans**.
2. Loans in **Under Review** status are listed with applicant details and score inputs.
3. Click a loan to view the full application:
   - Loan amount and purpose
   - Farm size, cooperative rating, sales score
4. Click **Approve** (set disbursement amount) or **Reject** (add reason).
5. Mark loans as **Repaid** when the farmer completes repayment.

---

### 9.4 Platform Analytics

1. Go to **Admin → Analytics**.
2. The analytics dashboard shows:
   - Total sales volume (value and units) over selectable time periods
   - Farmer performance rankings
   - Active users by role
   - Loan portfolio health (disbursed, repaid, overdue)
   - Logistics KPIs (average delivery time, completion rate)
   - Warehouse utilisation rates
3. Export reports as CSV for offline analysis.

## 10. Notifications

All users have access to the **Notifications** centre (bell icon, top navigation).

- Unread notifications appear with a red badge count.
- Click the bell to open the notification panel.
- Notifications cover: order updates, payment confirmations, loan decisions, logistics status changes, and admin alerts.
- Click **Mark All as Read** to clear the badge.
- SMS alerts are sent for critical updates (payment received, loan approved) via Africa's Talking integration.

## 11. Insurance Portal

1. Go to **Insurance** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Apply for Insurance** and select the coverage type:
   - Crop insurance (weather, pest, disease)
   - Asset / equipment insurance
3. Fill in the application:
   - Crop type, farm size, and location
   - Coverage period
   - Estimated asset value
4. Submit and await review from an insurance partner.
5. Track your policy status under **Insurance → My Policies**.

## 12. Equipment Listing & Hire

### Listing Equipment (Farmers / Owners)

1. Go to **Equipment Listing → Add Equipment**.
2. Provide:
   - Equipment name and type (e.g. Tractor, Combine Harvester, Irrigation Pump)
   - Daily / weekly hire rate (₦)
   - Location
   - Availability calendar
   - Photos and condition description
3. Submit the listing for review.

### Hiring Equipment (Farmers / Buyers)

1. Go to **Equipment Listing → Browse**.
2. Filter by equipment type, location, and availability date.
3. Select equipment and click **Request Hire**.
4. Agree on dates, pay the hire fee through the platform wallet or payment gateway.

## 13. Agricultural Advisory Hub

1. Go to **Inputs** or navigate to the Advisory section accessible from the dashboard.
2. Select your **crop** and **region** to personalise the advisory feed.
3. The feed surfaces:
   - Planting guides and seasonal calendars

## 15. Troubleshooting & FAQ

**Q: My product listing is not showing in the marketplace.**
A: Listings require admin approval after submission. This typically takes 24–48 hours on business days. Check **Product Listing → My Listings** for the current status.

**Q: My payment was deducted but the order shows as Pending.**
A: Payment processing can take a few minutes. Refresh the page. If the issue persists after 10 minutes, contact support with your payment reference number.

**Q: I cannot request a logistics pickup.**
A: Ensure you have a confirmed, approved product order linked to the request. Logistics requests can only be raised for confirmed orders.

**Q: My loan application shows "Rejected" but no reason.**
A: Click the application row to see the admin note. Common reasons include insufficient transaction history, incomplete cooperative registration, or requested amount exceeding the eligible limit.

**Q: The price intelligence page shows "No data for this crop/location."**
A: Not all crop-location combinations have sufficient market data yet. Try a nearby region or a broader category. New data feeds are added regularly.

**Q: I forgot my password and no reset email arrived.**
A: Check your spam/junk folder. If not found, ensure you are entering the exact email used during registration. Contact support if the issue persists.

**Q: How do I change my role?**
A: Go to **Account Settings → Role** and submit a role change request. Admin approval is required for role upgrades (e.g. Farmer → Warehouse Manager).

**Q: Is my financial data secure?**
A: Yes. All payment processing is handled by PCI-DSS compliant gateways (Paystack and Flutterwave). AgroLink never stores raw card numbers. All data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS.
